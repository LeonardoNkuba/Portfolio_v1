AT THE DOOR

People wanted to eat, but getting seated meant a wait of around 30 minutes. Managing waitlists and responding to questions are part of my daily work. That day brought those responsibilities into focus alongside the drinks service.

LOOKING AFTER GUESTS

I pay attention to what guests need, address concerns, and help create a welcoming dining experience—from the first greeting through their time with us.
